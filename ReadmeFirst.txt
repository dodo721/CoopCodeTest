Cooperative Innovations
=======================

There are two tests. Please do them in order.

The first is:
	CoopCodeTest.cpp

Instructions are included at the top of the file.
Spend at most an hour on this.

The second is:
	CoopAlgorithmTest.txt (with supporting documentation in EyeMeshData.cpp, Figure_1.jpg, Figure_2.jpg and Figure_3.jpg)

Spend as long as you want on this test, after having done what you can with CoopCodeTest.cpp.
Do not spend more than a normal working day in total, less time is fine. The aim of this is to show you're thinking and problem solving, not to produce shipping code. As mentioned in the instructions, please record a timelapse video. Seeing your working process in the video is _more_ important than the result.

Use any resources you like, except for asking someone else. Use Google, books, YouTube any references that you would normally to use to solve problems, just with the exception of asking someone else. If you're working doesn't show in the timelapse - for example if you draw some diagrams to figure through things, take pictures and include the pictures.
